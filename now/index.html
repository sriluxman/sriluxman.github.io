What has my attention now

Most of my energy goes into agentic and physical AI for the factory floor: intelligence that runs close to the machine, and the identity, governance and audit that make it trustworthy.

Updated November 11, 2026

  1. November 2026

    Sharpening a set of convictions about industrial AI: trust sets the pace of adoption, the model is the smallest part of the work, and no AI feature may leave a machine worse off than it is today. Two new essays and two notes.

  2. November 2026

    Thinking hard about simulation and digital twins as a source of rules, not pictures: an answer from a twin only counts once it has converged, and only pays once it changes a decision.

  3. July 2026

    Digging into physical AI and world models (the JEPA line of research) and what they could mean for the economics of machine intelligence. First essay: Scaling intelligence down.

  4. July 2026

    Wrote about agent identity on the machine floor: why agents working beside operators and technicians will need identity, scoped authority and traceability. Essay: Agents will need badges.

  5. June 2026

    Launched this site, with essays on platform trust, agentic engineering discipline, and cloud-decorated industrial software.
